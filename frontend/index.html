Literature search over 345M papers

BM25 ranking with a custom Block-Max WAND engine in C++, over the English OpenAlex corpus.

Try:

    How it works
    • The query is tokenized and stemmed the same way the 345M indexed works were.
    • A C++ engine scores it with BM25, and Block-Max WAND skips blocks of postings that cannot reach the top results.
    • Titles, authors and venues come from the OpenAlex API. Each result links to its OpenAlex record.
    Data: OpenAlex, June 2026 snapshot.
